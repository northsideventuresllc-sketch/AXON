When JB gives a "do not stop until everything is done" order, before ending any turn check: is there a next item still open? If yes, either keep going in the same turn or state plainly "continuing to item X now" and actually continue. A complete, accurate status report is still a stop if there's more on the list and nothing said about what's next.
