# Confirm Ponder/competitive-scan claims against a primary source

A viral-clip or social-post claim about a competitor, tool or trend is not evidence by itself — confirm it against a primary source (the project's own site/blog, its GitHub repo, an arxiv paper, or the platform's own docs) before logging it as a real finding. Log the verification method used alongside the claim, not just the claim text.
