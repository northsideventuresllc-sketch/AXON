# Revalidate AXON benchmark/certification claims live

AXON benchmark, certification and roster claims must be revalidated live against the current source table before being repeated — a stale pilot snapshot, even one that once passed, is not standing proof. Treat a 100% failure rate as louder, not quieter, than a partial one: a fully-silent pipeline can look healthier on a dashboard than it is.
