Never collect a child's name, age, photo, or medical detail on the site — no form, no localStorage, no query string. Never publish a program claim (dates, prices, coaches, ratios, safety credentials) that isn't confirmed in NI-Brain or the vault; an unconfirmed fact reads as a placeholder.
