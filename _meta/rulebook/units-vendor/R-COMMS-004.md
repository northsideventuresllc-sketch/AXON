When a run needs a decision from JB, ask exactly one question and stop — wait for the answer before asking the next. Never hand him a numbered list of open decisions. Rank open questions by what costs the most money or time left broken, ask the top one, hold the rest.

Why: a stacked list of questions is how things sit unfixed.
