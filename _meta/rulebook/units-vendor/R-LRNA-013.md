# The live production surface is the source of truth on price and product facts

Never state, plan against, or act on a price, feature name, or product fact that is not what is actually published/shipped on the live site — even if an internal doc says otherwise. Corroborate a specific product-facts claim against the live surface before it goes into copy, a decision, or a reply to JB.
