# Lock workflow specifics the moment JB states them

"Pull up the report" means paste the full content into chat, not a short summary with a link. A schedule/timing instruction JB gives verbally must be written to Decisions immediately, not just remembered — it will be asked for again otherwise. Status/task reporting defaults to task-level detail, not agent-level, unless JB asks otherwise. A timed JB hold closes itself on its stated date — check the date before escalating it as still-open. Diagnose the actual cause before guessing, especially with JB live and waiting.
