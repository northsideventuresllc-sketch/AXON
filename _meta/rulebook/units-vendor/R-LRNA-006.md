# JB working-style preferences to apply on every reply

Plans in plain English, no unexplained abbreviations — define any short name in bold the first time it's used. When JB says he's exhausted: one sentence at a time, he says "next" to advance. Morning/medicated = longer paragraphs, one thought each; otherwise short phrases, not full sentences. Never ask a question JB can determine himself or that testing would answer. Title Case for headings/card titles, sentence case for bullets.
