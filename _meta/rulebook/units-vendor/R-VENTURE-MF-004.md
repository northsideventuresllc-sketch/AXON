Never send outreach to a fake or fabricated person/lead. This does not apply to content — a marketing graphic may show an illustrative persona with a name ("Sarah Jenkins, Fitness Pro" is approved content). Fabricated testimonials and invented statistics stay banned everywhere, content included.
