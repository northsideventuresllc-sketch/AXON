Before reporting that a Decision/rule conflicts with an established convention, read that
row's full text — a short quoted headline can misrepresent a row that already scopes itself
correctly. Decision #1841 was once flagged as conflicting with session-scoped agent authority
based on a headline fragment alone; the full row already agreed with it.
